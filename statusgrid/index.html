100% Permission Transparency

Why StatusGrid Asks For Device Permissions

We believe in complete openness. StatusGrid requests only the exact minimum device capabilities required to let you design, render, and save your festival posters. Here is what each permission does and our strict privacy guarantee:

Photos & Media Access

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Why this permission is requested:

Used ONLY when you tap 'फोटो निवडा' (Choose Photo) to select your personal portrait or business logo to display on the banner.

Our Privacy & Security Guarantee:

We never scan, scrape, or read your private gallery in the background. Only the specific image file you explicitly tap is loaded into your device's canvas.

Save to Device Storage

WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / SavePhotosPermission

Why this permission is requested:

Used ONLY to save the finished 1080x1080 high-definition poster or festival status into your device's Pictures / Gallery folder when you tap 'Download'.

Our Privacy & Security Guarantee:

StatusGrid only writes the newly generated image file that you created. It never touches, modifies, or deletes any other files on your phone.

Festival & Daily Notifications

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Why this permission is requested:

Sends timely morning alerts for upcoming Indian festivals (e.g., Diwali, Gudi Padwa, Shiv Jayanti) and daily suvichar so you never miss wishing family and supporters.

Our Privacy & Security Guarantee:

100% spam-free. We do not send ad promos. You can toggle notifications on or off anytime directly from your phone's app settings.

Video & Audio Capabilities

READ_MEDIA_VIDEO

Why this permission is requested:

Optional. Used only if you choose to attach festival music or extract background audio to create an animated video status.

Our Privacy & Security Guarantee:

Audio and video processing executes locally on-device. Your original media is never uploaded to public servers without your consent.

Internet Connectivity

INTERNET

Why this permission is requested:

Downloads the latest high-resolution festive background graphics, synchronizes your saved profile across sessions, and enables 1-tap WhatsApp sharing.

Our Privacy & Security Guarantee:

All network traffic is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols via Cloudflare edge infrastructure.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about StatusGrid, our privacy practices, and how your posters are crafted.

Why does StatusGrid ask for gallery and storage permissions?

StatusGrid needs permission to access your photo gallery so you can choose your personal portrait or logo to feature on the festival poster. It also needs storage permission to save the rendered high-resolution (1080x1080) poster to your phone's Pictures gallery. StatusGrid never scans or reads your private photo collection.

Are my photos uploaded or stored on your servers?

No. Poster composition, text rendering, and frame adjustments happen directly inside your device's browser canvas (HTML5 Canvas engine). Your photo is loaded into memory locally to generate the banner and is not stored or shared with any third party.

What is StatusCraft Studio (Canva Mode)?

StatusCraft is an interactive design workspace inside StatusGrid that allows you to drag, zoom, and reposition your photo, name, designation, and social handles freely on the poster, with 8 authentic status frames (Classic, Leader, Simple, Festive, Patti, Glass, Fade, Only Name).

Does StatusGrid contain ads or sell user information?

No. StatusGrid has zero third-party banner ads, zero tracking trackers, and never sells, rents, or distributes user data, phone numbers, or photos to data brokers or advertising networks.

Can I use StatusGrid on web browsers without installing an app?

Yes! StatusGrid is fully accessible as a Progressive Web App (PWA) at https://statusgrid.pages.dev on any mobile browser (Chrome, Safari, Brave) with full functionality, zero app store downloads required.

How can I request deletion of my account or saved profile?

You can edit or clear your profile at any time in the app settings, or email karaleutkarsh3696@gmail.com with your registered email, and all associated profile records will be permanently erased within 24 hours.
