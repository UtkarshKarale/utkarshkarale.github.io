Frequently Asked Questions

How does FlowRupee track expenses without cloud servers?
FlowRupee uses a high-performance local SQLite database directly on your Android device. All transaction amounts, category tags, split calculations, and monthly budgets are stored in private local storage. No financial data ever hits a remote server.
Does FlowRupee require bank account logins or OTPs?
No. FlowRupee never asks for your internet banking credentials, debit card numbers, or SMS OTPs. You can log payments manually in under 3 seconds or allow local on-device transaction parsing without compromising your bank security.
How does group bill splitting work with flatmates or trips?
You can create unlimited groups for rent, groceries, trips, or dining out. FlowRupee's debt minimization algorithm calculates the exact minimum transactions required to settle up balances so you avoid awkward multiple payment reminders.
Is there any advertising or data sharing with credit bureaus?
None. FlowRupee is 100% ad-free and privacy-first. Your financial habits are never packaged, sold, or shared with third-party advertisers, loan lenders, or credit profiling platforms.
Can I use FlowRupee completely offline without Wi-Fi or mobile data?
Yes. FlowRupee is engineered offline-first. It functions seamlessly in remote locations, flight mode, or subway transits with zero network latency.
