Privacy Policy

Your financial records are personal. This policy explains what FlowRupee may process, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · App: FlowRupee · Service: JiApps

1. About FlowRupee

FlowRupee helps users record income and expenses, organise budgets and financial entries, and manage shared expenses. Some features may allow users to scan QR codes or open a separate UPI or payment app. FlowRupee is a record-keeping tool; unless a feature explicitly says otherwise, entries are user-provided and are not independently verified against bank records.

2. Information we may process

The information processed depends on the features you use and the version of the app. It may include:

  • Account information: name, email address, account identifier, account-verification status, and authentication or session data.
  • Financial records you enter: income, expenses, amounts, categories, dates, notes, budgets, and records you choose to keep for investments or loans.
  • Shared-expense information: friend or partner identifiers, split requests, amounts, notes, and request or settlement status needed to provide shared-expense features.
  • QR or camera information: QR-code content or images only when you use a relevant scanning or capture feature and grant the required device permission. The exact data processed depends on the feature.
  • Technical information: app version, diagnostic or error information, and basic connection or device information where generated by the app, hosting infrastructure, or included software development kits (SDKs).
  • Contacts, if enabled: if a version of the app asks for contact access, the in-app permission prompt should explain the purpose. How contacts are accessed, matched, uploaded, or kept on-device must match the actual implementation.

FlowRupee does not claim to read bank transaction SMS messages or access bank accounts as part of this policy. If such a feature is introduced, this policy, in-app disclosures, permissions, and the Google Play Data safety form must be reviewed and updated before release.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure your account, verify your email when required, save and synchronise your records, calculate and display budgets, support shared-expense requests, provide QR-related features, maintain service reliability, prevent misuse, and respond to support or privacy requests.

4. Where information is stored

Some app data may be stored locally on your device so the app can provide its intended functionality. Account and synchronised information may also be processed by FlowRupee's backend services. The service's actual storage, backup, and retention configuration must be reflected here before publication. Removing the app from your device may not delete data already synchronised to your account.

5. When information is shared

FlowRupee does not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed in limited circumstances:

  • With other users: information needed for a split request or shared-expense feature may be visible to the friends or participants you choose to interact with.
  • With service providers: infrastructure, authentication, delivery, diagnostics, or other providers may process information to operate features that are actually enabled.
  • With payment apps: if you choose to open a UPI or payment app, that app and the relevant payment providers handle the payment flow under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • For legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

The current launch version is intended to contain no in-app advertising. If advertising, analytics, or other SDKs are added, their data practices must be reviewed and this policy and the Google Play Data safety form updated as needed.

6. UPI and payment activity

FlowRupee may help prepare or open a payment request in a separate UPI app. Opening another app or returning to FlowRupee does not, by itself, prove that a payment succeeded. Do not share your UPI PIN, bank password, or one-time verification code with FlowRupee support.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used to protect information, including secure network connections, appropriate access controls, protected authentication and session handling, and secure password storage. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The safeguards described here must be verified against the deployed implementation.

When a valid account-deletion request is processed, associated account and user data should be deleted unless a specific legal obligation or permitted exception requires retention. Any backup-retention period must be accurately stated by the service operator.
