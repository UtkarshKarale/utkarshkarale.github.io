Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No, you do not need an account or to sign up to use Daymark. It works straight out of the box.

Where is my data stored?

All your event data is stored locally within your browser using Chrome's extension local storage APIs.

Does the extension track me or send my data to a server?

No. Daymark does not track your browsing history, and we do not operate a backend server to collect your data.

Can I backup and restore my events?

Yes! You can export your events and settings as a backup file from the extension menu and restore them whenever needed.

What happens if I uninstall the extension?

If you remove the extension or clear your browser's local storage, your event data will be permanently deleted unless you have exported a backup file first.
